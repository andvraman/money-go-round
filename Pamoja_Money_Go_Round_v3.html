Pamoja Money-Go-Round

PAMOJA Sub-Component 1.2 · how program money moves · v3 (draft)

1 · Money goes round

PMO-RALG funds the FI. The FI lends to CMGs. CMGs repay the FI, the FI pays PMO-RALG back, and PMO-RALG lends the money out again.

2 · When a CMG fails to repay

The FI tries to recover. If the loan is still unpaid or only partially repaid at the agreed days past due, the FI writes it down and stops recovery. PMO-RALG then pays its share of the loss.

3 · Rewards

PMO-RALG pays rewards to FIs and CMGs from a grant pool, for the first 2 years or until the pool runs out, whichever comes first. Grant money is not lent and does not come back.

Notes and glossary

Agreed

  • A CMG can have at most 2 loans; the 2nd only if the 1st is repaid in full and on time.
  • CMGs may withdraw loan funds in cash, and may repay in cash into the FI’s collection account at its bank, quoting an FI-issued reference number.
  • The FI pays back principal and interest into PMO-RALG’s collection account. PMO-RALG returns the FI’s majority share of the interest at regular intervals.
  • The FI gets fresh capital only when PMO-RALG recycles funds to it.
  • After a write-down the FI may not pursue recovery. The agreement can set penalties or suspension for confirmed cases.

Alternative for flow 1, step 4

  • Each quarter (or another agreed period), the FI pays only the principal and PMO-RALG’s share of the interest, and keeps its own share.

  • What happens to a CMG after its 2nd loan.
  • Cash repayments: can CMGs repay through a Wakala? Who bears any fees? Can the Wakala record the FI account and the reference number?
